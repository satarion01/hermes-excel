DATA HANDLING

Privacy

This page describes the public website and current Hermes Excel technical beta. It does not replace the policies of your Hermes deployment, selected model provider, or Microsoft.

Website and downloads

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and files are distributed through GitHub Releases. We add no analytics, advertising trackers, sign-in forms, or first-party cookies to these pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, request metadata, and other information under its privacy statement. Your language choice is saved in browser storage when available. The website itself does not read Excel workbooks or connect to the local helper.

Excel pane

Your selected Hermes receives chat messages and data required for permitted operations. Selection context is sent when explicitly attached to a message. With workbook access enabled, the agent can request bounded ranges and perform supported reads and writes; results and differences are returned through the integration.

Connection addresses and interface settings may be saved locally. Non-secret conversation-binding preferences may be saved in the workbook. Model-provider keys remain with the relevant Hermes deployment; this website does not request them.

The pane loads Office.js from Microsoft infrastructure. In remote mode, data is sent to your chosen server; even local Hermes may contact an external model provider. We therefore do not promise that data stays on your computer.

Retention and further processing

Chat history, logs, and data received by Hermes and its model provider are processed under those services’ settings and policies. This add-in does not determine their retention periods, training use, or deletion procedures. Contact your Hermes operator and model provider for these matters. Do not share confidential workbooks without appropriate permission.

Contact

The maintainer of this website and beta distribution is satarion01. You can open an issue about the website, but issues are public: do not include personal data or secrets. For deletion requests concerning Hermes data, contact the owner of the server you used.
